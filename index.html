What is Reflex Rush?

Reflex Rush is a free browser arcade with five short challenges: Quick Reflex, Color Rush, Fake Out, Speed Survival and Memory Flash. Each mode tests a different skill, from reaction speed to attention and recall. No account or download is required.

Five ways to play

  1. Quick Reflex: react when the green signal appears.
  2. Color Rush: tap the requested colour before time runs out.
  3. Fake Out: react to real GO signals and ignore fakes.
  4. Speed Survival: hit moving targets before they disappear.
  5. Memory Flash: remember and repeat an expanding colour sequence.

What is a good reaction time?

Reaction speed varies with attention, fatigue, device and input method. Browser-based measurements also include rendering and device input delay, so Quick Reflex scores are intended for entertainment and personal comparison rather than scientific assessment.

Scores and personal-best records are stored locally in your browser. They are not an externally verified leaderboard.

Tips

  • Use your dominant hand for better speed.
  • Stay relaxed — tensing up slows your reaction.
  • Do not try to anticipate the signal; that leads to false starts.
  • Retry a few times — reaction times vary moment to moment.
  • Better results on desktop with a physical mouse or keyboard.
