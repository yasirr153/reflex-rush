Online Reaction Time Test — Quick Reflex

Quick Reflex is a fast browser reaction test built around one simple challenge: wait for the signal, then tap as quickly as you can. It is designed for short sessions, personal bests and repeat attempts rather than scientific measurement.

Play Quick Reflex →

How to play

  1. Press Play and wait for the green signal.
  2. Do not tap early — a false start costs a life.
  3. Tap as soon as the signal appears.
  4. Complete the rounds and compare your score with your personal best.

Why play Quick Reflex?

  • Reaction speed can vary from attempt to attempt, so replaying gives you a better sense of your usual performance.
  • The test works without an account or download, making it easy to use on a phone, tablet or desktop browser.
  • The result is intended for entertainment and personal comparison. Device latency, display refresh and input method can affect browser measurements.

Tips for a better score

  • Keep your hand relaxed rather than tense.
  • Focus on the target area and avoid trying to predict the signal.
  • Use a physical mouse or keyboard on desktop if you want consistent input.
  • Try several rounds under similar conditions when comparing results.

Frequently asked questions

What is a good reaction time?

There is no single score that defines a good reaction time. Results depend on attention, fatigue, hardware and input method. Use Quick Reflex mainly to compare your own attempts.

Is this reaction time test free?

Yes. Quick Reflex is free to play in a modern web browser and does not require a download.

Can I use it on a phone?

Yes. The game is designed for touch and works on mobile and desktop browsers.
