Free Speed Tapping Game — Speed Survival

Speed Survival turns fast tapping into a short arcade challenge. Targets appear in different positions and disappear if you wait too long. Hit enough targets to finish the run while keeping your misses under control.

Play Speed Survival →

How to play

  1. Start the challenge and look for the target.
  2. Tap the target as quickly as you can.
  3. Targets move to new positions after each successful hit.
  4. A missed target counts against your limited misses.
  5. Finish the target sequence to complete the run.

Why play Speed Survival?

  • Changing target positions prevent the game from becoming a simple memorisation exercise.
  • The short format makes it useful for quick competitive sessions.
  • The combination of speed and accuracy creates a clear replay loop: finish, see your score, and try again.

Tips for a better score

  • Keep your pointer or finger near the play area.
  • Scan the whole field rather than staring at one position.
  • Avoid rushing so much that you miss the target completely.
  • Use a larger screen if small touch targets are difficult to hit accurately.

Frequently asked questions

What is Speed Survival?

It is a browser-based speed tapping game where you hit appearing targets before they disappear.

How do I avoid misses?

Scan the play area continuously and move quickly to each new target. Accuracy matters as much as speed.

Is it free?

Yes. Speed Survival is free to play without an account or download.
